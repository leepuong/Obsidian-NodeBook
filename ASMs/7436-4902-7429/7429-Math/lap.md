### Functions

$$a)\quad f: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R},\quad f(x)  = -3x +6 $$
Suppose that $y$ is the image of $x$, so that $y= -3x +6$
$$3x = 6-y$$
$$x = \frac {6-y}3$$
$$\Rightarrow \quad f^{-1}(y) = \frac{6-y}3 $$
$$\Rightarrow \quad f(x) = \frac{6-x}3 $$
